Market data, organized for research.

ACE Research Drive is a privately operated research utility for collecting and validating publicly available OKX market candlestick data. It uses Google Drive, with the account owner's authorization, to store research datasets and integrity manifests.

Public market data

Collect historical and incremental OHLCV candles from publicly available exchange endpoints for research and backtesting.

Account-controlled storage

Store research files in the authorized Google Drive account using scoped Google Drive API access.

Verification before use

Validate dataset coverage, checksums, duplicate candles, time gaps, and OHLC consistency.

How Google Drive access is used

With the operator's explicit authorization, ACE Research Drive requests the drive.file scope to manage the research files it creates or is authorized to access. It does not request unrestricted access to all Google Drive files.

The project is not a public trading service, does not offer investment recommendations, and does not accept user signups through this site.
