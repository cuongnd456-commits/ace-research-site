Privacy Policy

Effective: September 28, 2026. This policy describes the current, privately operated ACE Research Drive research utility. It should be updated if its features or data practices change.

1. Purpose and data accessed

ACE Research Drive collects publicly available market candlestick data for research and stores research outputs in a Google Drive account authorized by the operator. It requests Google's drive.file permission, allowing it to create and manage files it creates or files the operator explicitly authorizes the app to access. It does not request unrestricted access to the operator's other Drive files.

2. Use of information

Google Drive access is used to upload, read, synchronize and validate research files, such as market-data partitions, coverage manifests, and reports. Research automation may execute through GitHub Actions. The public website does not provide a sign-up form or ask visitors to connect their Google accounts.

3. Storage and security

Research files are stored in the operator's own Google Drive. The operator controls access and retention in that account. Google OAuth credentials used by automation are configured as private execution secrets; credentials and research data must not be placed in this public website's repository. Storage and processing also depend on the relevant Google Drive and GitHub infrastructure.

4. Sharing, sale, and advertising

ACE Research Drive does not sell Google user data, share it with advertising networks, or use it for targeted advertising. Data is processed through Google Drive and the research automation infrastructure only as needed to operate the research utility, subject to those providers' applicable terms.

5. Retention and deletion

The authorized account owner can remove research files from Google Drive, disable research automation, and revoke the app's Google account access at Google Account permissions. Revoking access prevents new authorized operations but does not automatically delete files or historical execution logs already retained by the service providers. The owner manages those separately.

6. Website visitors

This static website contains no visitor account registration, advertising trackers or custom analytics scripts. The hosting provider may process ordinary connection and security logs under its own policies.

7. Contact and changes

For privacy questions or requests about this privately operated project, contact the developer at cuongnd456@gmail.com. This policy will be updated if the project's actual data handling changes.
